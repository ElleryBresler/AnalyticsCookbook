# Rosie's Famous Chocolate Sour-Cream Layer Cake

**Contributed by:** Alex McKenzie
**Serves:** 12  |  **Time:** 2.5 hours

## Why this one

I make this cake every year for my wife's birthday. It's a rich, moist chocolate layer cake with a sour cream tang and a fudge frosting that has to be made in a blender. I made it for her once around 2010 and she's never looked back.

## Ingredients

**Cake**
- 4 ounces unsweetened chocolate
- 2 cups sugar
- 1 1/2 cups flour
- 3/4 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup hot strong brewed coffee
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 2 large eggs

**Fudge Frosting (doubled)**
- 8 ounces unsweetened chocolate
- 1 1/2 cups evaporated milk
- 2 cups sugar

## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 345°F. Lightly grease two 8-inch layer cake pans, line with parchment circles, then grease again and dust with flour.
2. Melt chocolate in the top of a double boiler over simmering water, then turn off heat.
3. Sift the sugar, flour, baking soda, and salt together into a large mixing bowl.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the hot coffee, sour cream, and vegetable oil.
5. With the mixer on low speed, add the coffee mixture to the dry ingredients in a stream and mix until blended, about 35 seconds. Stop to scrape the bowl several times.
6. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing on medium-low after each addition until smooth, about 15 seconds each, scraping the bowl each time. Add the melted chocolate and mix until the batter is uniform in color.
7. Divide the batter evenly between the two prepared pans.
8. Bake about 30 minutes, until the cake springs back to the touch and a tester inserted in the center comes out dry.
9. Cool completely before frosting.
10. For the frosting: melt chocolate in the top of a double boiler and cool slightly.
11. Blend the evaporated milk and sugar in a blender on medium speed for 2 seconds.
12. Add the chocolate to the sugar mixture and blend on high speed until thick and shiny, about 2 minutes. The blender's sound will change as it thickens.
13. Spoon the frosting into a bowl and let sit at room temperature about 30 minutes, then cover and let set 1 hour before frosting the cake.

## Notes

Frosting must be made in a blender, not a stand mixer or food processor; the blending action is what thickens it. Do not refrigerate the frosting. Do not skip the parchment circles in the cake pans; sour cream cakes stick easily. The original frosting recipe (4 oz chocolate, 3/4 cup evaporated milk, 1 cup sugar, about 1 3/4 cups total) isn't enough to fill and frost a two-layer cake; doubling it, as written above, is correct.
