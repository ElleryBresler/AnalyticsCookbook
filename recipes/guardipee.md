# <Orange Fluff>

**Contributed by:** <Megan Guardipee>
**Serves:** <8>  |  **Cook Time:** <40>

## Why this one

Once you have it, you will want it again! This is my go to for family picnics, potlucks, holiday dessert, and just because! It is easy, fast and under $10. 

## Ingredients

- 1 orange jello mix 3 oz. 
- 1 instant vanilla pudding 3.4oz package
- 2 cans of Mandarin oranges
- 1 cup of Hot boiled water
- 1/2 cup of cold water
- 1 bag of mini marshmallows 10oz. 
- 1 tub of whip cream thawed

## Steps

1. Cook Jello in one cup of bowling water in a large mixing bowl, mix until gelatin is completely dissolved.
2. Add the cold water and place in the fridge for 30 minutes or until mixture is thickened slightly.
3. Take the bowl out of fridge, stir in the instant pudding mix until smooth and well combined. Then back in fridge for another 20 minutes.
4. Take bowl out of fridge again, uncover, next fold in whip cream until fully combined and the texture is smooth and fluffy. Add in fruit and marshmallows. 
5. Cover, Put back in fridge 2-4 hours. 
6. Give the Orange fluff gently stir before serving if needed, Serve the fluff cold and enjoy!  


## Notes
Best if kept in fridge overnight. 
Enjoy! Keep cool. 
Refrigerate leftovers, covered for up to 3-4 days.
