# Orange Peach Smoothie

**Contributed by:** Janessa Chaplin
**Serves:** 4  |  **Time:** 3 minutes

## Why this one

I originally found this orange-peach smoothie recipe online and have since memorized it. I make it whenever I want a quick breakfast or refreshing snack. It is worth trying because it tastes great and requires very little time or effort.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups orange juice
- 2 cups frozen peach slices
- 1 cup peach yogurt
- 1-2 tablespoons honey

## Steps

1. Add the ingredients into the blender in the order listed; orange juice, frozen peaches, yogurt, and honey.
2. Blend it until everything is smooth, creamy, and combined. It takes about 1 minute in my blender.

## Notes

I use plain non-fat greek yogurt and it still tastes great. Also can try it with orange juice that has pineapple or banana.
