# <Dessert Empanadas>

**Contributed by:** <Ecy Flores>
**Serves:** <18>  |  **Time:** <60>

## Why this one

My mom has made empanadas ever since I can remember and they're delicious. I made three batches for friendsgiving a couple of years ago and left with just the containers.

## Ingredients

- 2 3/4 cups flour
- 1/2 cup butter
- 1/2 cup vegetable shortening
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 6 tablespoons of beer or soda
- 1/2 teaspoon cinnnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla

## Steps
### Empanada Dough:

1. Combine flour, sugar, butter, and vegetable shortening in a large bowl.
2. Mix the ingredients with your hands until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
3. Then, add the ground cinnamon and vanilla extract and gradually add the dark soda/beer drink mix until the dough comes together. 
4. Form it into a ball, wrap it in plastic wrap, and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes. This step relaxes the gluten in the dough, making it easier to roll out without shrinking or tearing.

### Assembly:
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F
2. With the help of a rolling pin, roll out the chilled dough on a lightly floured surface to about ⅛ inch thickness. Using a round cutter, cut out circles.
Another option is to separate the dough into 18 small balls and press each ball with the help of your tortilla press.
3. Now, place a spoonful of the filling in the center of each empanada disc. 
4. Fold the dough over the filling, creating a half-moon shape. Seal the edges by pressing them with a fork.
5. Place the empanaditas on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper and Bake in your preheated oven for 20 minutes.
6. After 20  minutes in the oven, your dessert empanadas may still look a little pale; this is ok, remove them from the oven.
7. Allow a few minutes until you can touch them, and place them on a plate or small bowl with sugar. You must do this step as soon as possible and sprinkle the sugar evenly, or the sugar will not stick to them. 
## Notes

- This recipe can be customized with your favorite filling, like jams, caramel sauce, apple filling, or dulce de leche mixed with nuts or fresh fruit. 
- If using your own filling, add a thickening agent like cornstarch to prevent it from becoming too runny during baking. Some people mix in one egg yolk to store-bought jams or dulce de leche to avoid it from oozing out at baking time. 
- If your filling is too runny, lightly brush the empanadas with the egg wash before closing them.
- Make sure the butter is cold to achieve a better consistency.
- It is important that you gradually add the dark soda to achieve the right consistency. Too much liquid can make the dough sticky.
- Additional spices can be added to the sugar to coat your warm empanadas, like a few tablespoons of ground cinnamon for extra flavor
