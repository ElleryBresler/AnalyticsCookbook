# Homemade Pizza Dough

**Contributed by:** Ellery Hill(Bresler)
**Serves:** 12|  **Time:** 30

## Why this one

We love pizza for a quick easy meal. We usually have all the ingriedents on hand and toppings are easy to come by. Homemade pizza dough is a cheap and easy thing to make ahead of time. This is not my recipe as I normally just find one on the internet and create it. 

## Ingredients

- 1 cup warm water
- 1 Tablespoon of granulated sugar
- 1 Tablespoon active dry yeast
- 1 Tablespoon olive oil
- 2 cups to 2 ½ cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp salt

## Make the Pizza Dough

1. Combine warm water, yeast and sugar in a large mixing bowl and stir to combine.
2. Let mixture sit for 5 minutes, or until it becomes frothy and bubbles form.
3. Gently stir in olive oil.
4. Add 2 cups of flour and salt and mix with a spatula until a ball begins to form (dough will still be slightly sticky). Add more flour as needed to form a dough ball.
5. Transfer to a floured surface and knead into a smooth dough, adding up to ½ cup extra flour if needed.


## Bake The Pizza

1.Preheat the oven to 425 degrees Fahrenheit 
2. Roll the dough into your desired shape and put it on a piece of parchment paper.
3. Transfer pizza to desired cooking tray (pizza tray, cookie sheet, etc.)
4. Poke the bottom of the pizza with a fork several times to avoid bubling. 
5. Add pizza sauce, cheese and toppings of choice.
6. Brush olive oil onto the crust of the pizza.
7. Bake for 13-16 minutes, or until the bottom of the crust is golden brown.


## Notes

I generally recommend to check the crust to see if it is golden. Another trick is to poke a toothpick into the crust to make sure it comes out with no dough.
