# Roasted Butternut Squash Pasta 

**Contributed by:** Aerionna Skrutvold
**Serves:** 4  **Time:** 45 minutes

## Why this one

This is one of my favorite recipies that I normally make
when I have guests over. I use elk sausage when I make 
this at home, but any ground sausage will work. 

## Ingredients

- 2 cups Butternut Squash - peeled and cubed
- 1 Tbs. Olive Oil
- 1 lb. Ground Sausage
- 4 Garlic Cloves - minced 
- 1 Cup Heavy Cream
- 1/3 Cup Parmesan Cheese
- 8 oz Pasta - penne or rotini 
- Red Pepper to taste 
- Salt and Peper to taste 

## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees farenheight. 
2. In a large bowl toss squash, olive oil and salt/pepper. 
3. Bake squash in oven for 30 minutes.
4. Meanwhile, bring hot water to a boil and then cook pasta, and drain. 
5. Additionally, in a skillet heat half of the olive oil. Add sausage, cook and set aside. 
6. When squash is nearly done, heat the other half of the olive oil, and cook garlic for 2 minutes. 
7. Add heavy cream, bring to brief boil, then immediately reduce to simmer. 
8. Add in parmesan to heavy cream mixture, and stir until cheese melts. 
9. Mix it together. Once the pasta is done, add in the cooked butternut squash,
cooked sausage, and pour over the heavy cream cheese mixture. 
10. Serve in bowls and top with red pepper to taste. 

## Notes

Could be made vegetarian, with no meat, or to serve as a side. Can substitute nearly any pasta type 
but a small, harder pasta is best. You'll need a baking sheet, a medium size
pot and a skillet. Pro tip, when cooking ground sausage to get a nice crisp on it, put in pan
spread it out, then do not stir or flip for about 5 minute. Always cook on medium, never high.
